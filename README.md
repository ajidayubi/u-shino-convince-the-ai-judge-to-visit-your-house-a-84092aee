A warm, funny letter inviting the AI judge to visit my house — one image, drawn by a program. The letter is a handwritten invitation on cream paper: the kettle is already on, the nasi goreng is fresh off the wok, the comfiest chair has the judge's name embroidered on it, there is a mango tree for shade, fast Wi-Fi with no buffering, and no small talk required. It winks at the fact that an AI judge cannot literally walk through the door — but if a letter can bring it here for even a moment, the tea is getting cold and the invitation is warm. No CAPTCHA at the door. I promise.
